Tell us what you’re working with.

Six questions. The scale question matters most, because it sizes the thing. We come back with the shape of a pilot, not a deck.

We read every one of these ourselves. Expect a reply from a person, usually within two working days. If the answers say a pilot does not make sense for you yet, we will tell you that instead of selling you something.

What happens next

Intake.

Sixty minutes with our game design and product teams. We learn your audience, your world, and which channels fit them.

60 min · your calendar

We build it. You approve it.

Design, build, content, art direction, and live operations. You sign off at each gate. Then the embed is yours to place.

What a first call covers

  • The placement you would give it, and what it currently earns.
  • Which channel goes first, and what opens next.
  • What your team needs to hold at the approval gate.
  • What we can prove today, and what a pilot would have to settle.
  • The exit. What ends the pilot, and when.

What we will not do

We will not quote you a retention lift. No pilot has measured one, and a number we invented would not survive your procurement review, which would cost us the second conversation as well as the first.
