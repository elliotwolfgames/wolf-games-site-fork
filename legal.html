What we collect, what we claim, and what we will not do.

01

What runs on your properties

A static bundle. No player-facing servers of ours sit between your audience and the game.

02

What we collect

Play events: what was started, what was finished, how long it took, what was answered. No account is required and none is offered in a pilot.

03

What the play data does for you

Play events answer one question: is this working, for this audience, this week. That signal is shared between your team and ours, and it compounds. Every edition that runs teaches the next one what your audience actually finishes, so the vault gets sharper for them specifically. Play telemetry never enriches a third-party profile, is never sold, and is never joined to an advertising identity graph.

The event log is the source of truth for both of us. You read the same events we read, which is the only way a retention number survives your own scrutiny.

04

Children

We do not build for audiences under thirteen and we do not collect from them.

05

Consent

No consent wall, because there is nothing behind one. First-party analytics only.

06

How we label claims

  • Proven. We have measured it or it is a plain fact about what we operate.
  • We expect. A design target. Reasoned, not measured.
  • Our bet. Unproven. Stated so you can decide whether to test it with us.
