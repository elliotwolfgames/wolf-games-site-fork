Not a new idea

The daily habit is the oldest working idea in media.

The crossword, the horoscope, the Monday quiz. People do not schedule time for content, they schedule it for a ritual, and a ritual is the one thing in this business that still compounds. Most partners have already had the internal conversation about building one. It tends to stop in the same place. A game on a cadence is not a project, it is a standing production line, and almost nobody is staffed to run one on top of the work they already do. Nor should they be.

That is the part we take off you, across as many channels as your audience will hold.

What changed

The audience is still there. The road to them is closing.

Search is becoming an answer surface rather than a referral engine. When an AI summary appears, people click a traditional result on 8 percent of visits, against 15 percent when it does not, and only 1 percent click a source cited inside the summary. Across more than 2,500 sites, Google organic referrals fell 33 percent in a single year, and publishers surveyed expect another 43 percent decline over the next three.

Nobody is coming back through the front door the way they used to. Direct habit is what is left, and it is worth more than it has ever been worth.
