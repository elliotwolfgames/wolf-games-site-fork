Where we came from

Wolf Games is the game studio inside Wolf Entertainment, the company behind Law & Order. Procedural television is the discipline of making self-contained stories on a schedule, at a quality bar that does not move, for years at a time. A vault of daily games is the same job on a tighter clock across several channels at once.

01

What we built

An engine that resolves fresh editions every day, for every channel, for every surface a partner runs. A house of art directions we can tune or rebuild per brand. And a production process that gates every edition before it ships. The AI does the volume. Writers and showrunners decide what is good. Neither half works alone and we are not interested in pretending otherwise.

Who is in the room

Over twenty writers, showrunners, game designers and AI engineers out of film, television and games. We move fast, we ship on schedule, and we hold a prestige bar on subject matter that most of this category does not bother with.

Why now

Daily games became some of the most valuable attention a brand or a publisher can own, while much of the industry was still calling them a nice-to-have. The gap is not creative and it is not technical. Daily games are a standing production line, and almost nobody can run one, let alone several. We can, and we would rather run them under your brand than compete with you for the same audience.
