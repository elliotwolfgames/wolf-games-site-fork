The production line.

Four stages run every day, on a schedule, for every channel in the vault, whether or not anyone is watching. Your team sits at the gate and approves. Nothing goes live that you have not cleared.

One pipeline, run once a day, for every channel in the vault.

Stage 01

Plan

We resolve what today’s edition is for each channel, from the story skeleton and the material you have given us.

Stage 02

Compile

Writers, designers and the engine build the edition. Art direction is applied from your kit.

Your approval Stage 03

Gate

Your team reviews and approves. Nothing passes this stage without a yes.

Stage 04

Drop

The edition goes live on every surface at once. No deploy, no release, no work on your side.

The gate is not a formality and it is not automated. A person on your team clears every edition before an audience sees it.

The vault, not a title

You are not buying one game.

A channel is one game with one continuing story and its own host, and someone subscribes to the channel rather than to the site. The vault holds a set of them, so a person who bounces off one mechanic still finds one that fits. Every channel runs the same daily pipeline out of the same source material.
