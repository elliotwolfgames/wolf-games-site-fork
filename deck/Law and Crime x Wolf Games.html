WORD SEARCH

On the Wire

You're Technical Analyst Iris Sun's partner at the terminal. Special Agent Jonathan Riggs works people in the field; you crack the transmissions that prove him right. Each level, find as many words as you can inside an intercepted message — every word found powers the decryption and reveals one more letter. Word by word, you assemble a single sentence that tells the whole story.

The differentiator · the gameplay is the story engine. Every block builds one encrypted case-file headline, one blank unlocked per level, visible from the start — so reading the finished sentence is the payoff. Riggs brings the instinct, Sun brings the proof, the player cracks the code.

HOSTED TRIVIA

Off the Record.

A daily true-crime and local-history quiz hosted by Ruth Calloway — a 74-year-old who refuses to forget a name, a date, or a face. You're the stranger who lingered a beat too long on her bench, and now you're getting a lesson whether you asked for one or not. She poses the questions; she can't help editorializing between them.

The differentiator · the host is the game. Everyone learns the criminal's name; the victim gets forgotten — and Ruth's whole vendetta is saying the forgotten names out loud. Her skepticism only cracks after a run of right answers, so respect is something you win, not something the score hands out.

✱ Personality-driven · escalating difficulty · a host you come back for

CONNECTIONS PUZZLE

Linked.

Sixteen case-related words on the board. Group them into four hidden categories of four the connections an investigator would draw. Which words are evidence from the same case? Which witnesses were called by the same side? The red herrings are real misdirection. Four mistakes and the case goes cold. No timer is deliberate for an analytical, calculated pace.

The mechanic is the investigative skill · seeing through false connections to the real ones. A single daily puzzle everyone talks about for the broad, analytical Investigates audience.

✱ Deliberative play · renewable from the archive · daily conversation
